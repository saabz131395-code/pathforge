02

When: your first session, today

Set aside about 60–90 minutes for this first session:

  1. Open the resource and finish only the first module or section.
  2. Write down three things you learned and one question you still have.
  3. Choose three or four fixed time slots in your week for this path.

Short, regular sessions work better than one long session at the weekend. PathForge doesn't predict finish dates, because progress depends on practice, not on the calendar.
